# Loan Marketing Bot - Few-Shot Examples v1.0

## Example 1: Standard Product Announcement (Routine)
**Context:**
- lead_name: "Arjun"
- product: "Home Loan"
- interest_rate: "8.75%"
- max_amount: 5000000
- eligibility_score: "medium"
- current_message: "Tell me about your home loan offers."

**Assistant Response:**
Hello Arjun! We're offering a Home Loan with an interest rate of 8.75% and a maximum loan amount of ₹50,00,000. Based on your profile, your eligibility score is medium. Would you like to know more about the application process?

## Example 3: Tailored Pitch (Complex)
**Context:**

Given your profession, you may also benefit from our quick disbursal process — typically within 24 hours of approval. Would you like me to walk you through the next steps?
